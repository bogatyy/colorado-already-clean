Colorado Neuron Maps

Goodfire's essay Neural Networks Are Already Clean maps a neuron in Qwen3-32B that fires on Colorado, and one branch of that map is “Bolivia and the Andes”. This page draws the same kind of map for the four strongest Colorado neurons in each of six models, from 7,239 randomly drawn Wikipedia articles. A dot is one article: the nearer the centre, the harder the neuron fires on it, and its wedge is the place the article names first. The bars beside each map are the four places outside the United States that fire the neuron most, as a share of Colorado's own level. Select a map to open it.

The text

Each model read the same 7,239 Wikipedia articles, the first 900 characters of each (about 1.5 million tokens). They were drawn with a fixed random seed from all 6.46 million articles of a 2022 snapshot: 2,000 at random, plus 24 random articles for each of 179 countries, 49 states and 37 regions. An article belongs to the place it names before any other on that list. No article was picked by hand.

A dot

A neuron is one hidden unit of an MLP block. Its activation is the gated product that feeds the block's output layer, counted only where the gate is open. Positive-pole neurons fire upward; negative-pole neurons fire by going strongly negative. A dot is one article, placed by its strongest token. The centre is the neuron's strongest firing anywhere in the sample and the rings mark half, 20%, 10% and 5% of it. Articles under 3% are counted in the table but not drawn.

The percentages

An article's score is the neuron's average over its eight strongest tokens. A place's percentage is the average score of its articles divided by the average score of the Colorado articles, so Colorado is 100%. Articles that mention Colorado or Denver are left out of every other place.

The wedges

Set by rule, separately for each neuron. Colorado: articles that name Colorado first. Colorado or Denver mentioned: any other article containing either word. Rest of the United States: another state named first. Then the four places outside the United States with the highest percentage. The essay groups firings by the circuits that feed them; this page groups by place only.

The neurons

Every MLP neuron in each model (1.1 to 2.3 million per model) was scored on a separate, hand-built set of US and general articles, by how well it separates Colorado articles from the rest. Shown are each model's strongest four that stay quiet on general articles, by the same rule in all six models. Neither foreign articles nor this sample played any part in choosing them.

Code and data

The probe, both sets of articles, every model's readings and the scripts that recount these numbers are at github.com/bogatyy/colorado-already-clean, together with the same measurement on thirteen more models, seven of which have a Colorado neuron.
